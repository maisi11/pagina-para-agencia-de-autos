## Brand & Style

The design system projects an aura of precision engineering, luxury, and high-performance reliability. Tailored for discerning automotive buyers, collectors, and modern dealerships, the visual language balances aerodynamic minimalism with high-contrast mechanical confidence.

- **Brand Personality**: Sophisticated, trustworthy, technologically progressive, and authoritative.
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Modern High-Contrast Minimalist combined with Tonal Engineering. Clean platinum and pure white presentation planes sit anchored over deep carbon foundations, enriched by electric racing blues and high-conversion emerald signals.
- **Emotional Response**: Evokes the prestige and tactile satisfaction of stepping onto an architectural showroom floor, instilling total transactional confidence and immediate visual clarity.

## Colors

The color palette reflects automotive precision: deep graphite chassis tones, aerodynamic electric blues, tactile transaction greens, and pure platinum surfaces.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Carbon / Slate 900. Governs authoritative titles, key structural frames, prominent navigation, and dark interface anchors.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Electric Racing Blue. Directs technical focus, vehicle specifications, active interactive states, links, and system notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Emerald Conversion. Dedicated strictly to buyer-action pathways, active inventory statuses ("Disponible"), and direct-to-advisor WhatsApp contact channels.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Structural Slate. Manages borders, secondary spec metadata (mileage, transmission, fuel type), and muted copy.
- **Surface Roles**: Canvas rests on `#F8FAFC` (Platinum Silver) with card bodies elevated in `#FFFFFF` (Pure Gloss White), bordered by subtle `#E2E8F0` hairline strokes.
- **Semantic Badges**:
  - *Disponible*: Emerald 50 background (`#ECFDF5`), Emerald 700 text (`#047857`), subtle `#A7F3D0` border.
  - *Pausada / Reservada*: Amber 50 background (`#FFFBEB`), Amber 800 text (`#92400E`), `#FDE68A` border.
  - *Vendido*: Slate 100 background (`#F1F5F9`), Slate 600 text (`#475569`), `#CBD5E1` border.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes Plus Jakarta Sans exclusively to deliver high-velocity geometric impact with flawless functional legibility.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set with deliberate negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (700/800) to emulate high-end automotive monograms and vehicle badges.
- **Price Tags**: Receive bold tabular styling to emphasize monetary figures cleanly without character shifts during filtering or currency calculations.
- **Technical Specs**: Rendered with increased letter spacing (`0.06em`) in uppercase format for badges (`badge-spec`) to create sharp machine-readable detail chips.
- **Body & Editorial**: Balanced at regular weight with open line heights to maintain effortless scanning across technical specification sheets and finance terms.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural rhythm relies on a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-angle imagery, specifications tables, and filter sidebars without visual crowding.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 3rem (`48px`) margins with a maximum container width of `1440px`. Filter sidebar spans 3 columns; vehicle inventory grid spans 9 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.5rem (`24px`) margins. Sidebar collapses to a horizontal filter drawer or sticky bottom sheet.
- **Mobile (Under 768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1rem (`16px`) outer canvas margin.
- **Component Padding Scale**:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Fine metadata chip gap, icon-to-text separation.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Badge interior padding, spec pill inline margins.
  - `space-md` (16px): Input field interior, card content container padding.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Card boundary padding, filter group separation.
  - `space-xl` (40px): Section vertical cadence, grid row offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines tactile ambient shadows with high-definition micro-borders, simulating calibrated glass showroom partitions and solid vehicular panels.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC` base surface without elevation or drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Static Panels)**: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns)**: Elevated vehicle cards on hover transition upwards by `-3px` with shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars & Modals)**: Full overlay planes and floating price summaries utilize: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Glass Accents**: Sticky header navigation and image carousel metadata overlays utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a 90% tinted background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` or deep carbon `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses modern calibrated curves that echo premium automotive cockpit design and streamlined sheet metal.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Inputs, buttons, technical spec pills, and status badges.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px)**: Vehicle cards, image gallery thumbnails, and search modal frames.
- **Extra-Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px)**: Hero visual containers, customer testimonial callouts, and bottom navigation sheets.
- **Full Pill Shape (`9999px`)**: Primary filter chips, floating WhatsApp quick-contact triggers, and mileage tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Transaction (WhatsApp / Contact)**: Background `#10B981`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`. Hover shifts to `#059669` with an elevation increase. Contains an inline SVG messaging icon on the left.
- **Secondary Action (View Details / Reserve)**: Background `#0F172A`, foreground `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#1E293B`.
- **Tertiary / Racing Outline**: Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#2563EB`, text `#2563EB`. Hover triggers `#EFF6FF` background.

### Vehicle Cards
- **Structure**: Aspect ratio `16:10` image container with top badges (Status and Year) anchored using absolute positioning (`0.75rem` offset).
- **Body**: Below photo, includes model title (`headline-sm`), trim level (`body-sm` in `#64748B`), and an inline horizontal list of mechanical specs (Mileage, Transmission, Engine) styled with micro-icons.
- **Footer**: Distinct hairline separator (`#F1F5F9`). Holds bold `price-lg` in carbon `#0F172A` paired alongside the compact WhatsApp direct CTA.

### Status Badges & Spec Pills
- **Vehicle Status**: Micro-pill (`roundedness: 2`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`) using uppercase `badge-spec`. Color-coded dynamically based on availability status.
- **Filter Pills**: Interactive filter pills with `#F1F5F9` neutral background, changing to solid `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text when selected.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, text `body-md`. Focus state introduces a high-contrast electric blue ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)` with a `#2563EB` border.
- **Range Sliders (Price & Mileage)**: Deep slate rail (`#E2E8F0`), electric blue filled track (`#2563EB`), with solid white circular grab handles cast with Level 2 elevation.

### Additional Automotive Components
- **Spec Grid**: A 4-column compact specification block for vehicle detail views highlighting 0-100 km/h, Horsepower, Fuel Economy, and Drivetrain via structured slate label-value pairings.
- **Comparison Drawer**: Docked bottom-shelf displaying up to 3 pinned vehicles with thumbnail, title, price, and instant side-by-side spec differentiation.
